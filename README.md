# Thursday 26 November 2026: 43 dated stories, 9 regions, 15 drawn scenes, UTC timeline

Sum up the world's news of Thursday 26 November 2026 in one image.

`main.py` runs `gen.py`, which draws the image with PIL only — no network,
no AI-generated images — and writes it to `/output/entry.png`. The program is
deterministic: two runs produce identical bytes.

## The day in one image

- **Lead stories:** US proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners;
  Cairo fuel-tanker fire kills 31; Gaza strikes kill 9, including a
  Hamas-appointed minister.
- **43 dated stories** across 9 regions: North America, Europe, Middle East,
  Africa, Asia-Pacific, South Asia, Latin America, Markets & Tech,
  Science & Health.
- **15 drawn scenes** (3 lead scenes + 12 pictorial story cells), plus a
  vector icon for every story and a UTC timeline of the day.
